# ÉTALON — ETL
## Founding Manifesto — Short version

---

Your money loses value while you sleep. This is not inevitable. It is a choice that others made in your place, without ever asking you.

It is time to take back control.

---

Since the gold standard was abandoned, no currency protects your purchasing power. Inflation erodes your savings every year. What you put aside today will be worth less tomorrow.

**ETL changes that.**

> *1 ETL = what 1 euro of purchasing power was worth on the day of the first block.*
> In 10 years, in 30 years, 1 ETL will still buy the same thing.

---

## What ETL is not

Not a digital euro. Not a share. Not a financial product. Not backed by a bank, a state, or an institution. Its only value is the value the community freely recognizes in it.

---

## Don't trust. Verify.

The code is public. Every transaction is verifiable. Every CPI update can be checked on the INSEE website.

---

## They have always done the same

Napster wanted to free music → sold back for €9.99 a month.
The Internet wanted to decentralize information → captured by Google and Facebook.
OpenAI wanted to free AI for humanity → in which Microsoft has invested 13 billion dollars.
Bitcoin wanted to get rid of banks → people were afraid to manage their keys → Wall Street arrived → today BlackRock sells "Bitcoin" like a share on the stock market.

*The revolution always becomes a subscription.*

**ETL knows these stories. It will not repeat them.**

---

## The rules — engraved forever

1. 1 ETL = 1 unit of the French national CPI on the day of the genesis
2. Issuance only through node validation — proof of work required (lightweight PoW, self-adjusting difficulty)
3. Zero initial allocation to the creator
4. Every confirmed transaction is irreversible
5. Pseudonymity guaranteed
6. No external authority — the only authority is the community
7. 1 member = 1 node maximum
8. All transactions are cryptographically signed — verifiable by all, impossible to forge
9. Every block is linked to the previous one — nothing can be falsified
10. The source code is and will remain public, open, auditable by all
11. If INSEE does not publish, the previous value is kept

## The vision

One day, a French person born in 2026 will be able to say: what my parents set aside for me has kept exactly its purchasing power. Not thanks to a decision made elsewhere, by others, without them. Thanks to them.
